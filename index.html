Key Tips
  • Octopus Card: Get at HKG arrival hall. Covers all HK MTR, buses, trams, shops — and Shenzhen Metro if you top up Mainland wallet.
  • Alipay / WeChat Pay: Link a foreign card before mainland China entry. Most vendors only accept QR payment.
  • Forbidden City (Beijing): Book on official WeChat mini-program, up to 7 days ahead (~€8). Sells out. Book the evening you arrive.

ZhangjiajieAvatar Mountains Day 1 · 魅力湘西
🏔 Wulingyuan Park · Bailong Elevator · Yuanjiajie · Golden Whip Stream · 魅力湘西 Show
07:00
Enter park early. Buy 2-day pass (~€33). Head straight to Bailong Elevator (world's tallest outdoor lift, 326m). Queue early.

Yuanjiajie Scenic Area — the Avatar landscape. Enormous sandstone pillars wrapped in hanging forest rising from deep misty valleys. The "Hallelujah Mountain" is here.

17:30
Exit park. Early dinner near the 魅力湘西 theatre area — local Tujia cuisine.
20:00
魅力湘西 (Charm of Western Hunan) — spectacular indoor show depicting Tujia and Miao minority culture through acrobatics, song, dance and traditional ceremony. One of China's best ethnic minority performances. ~90 min. Book tickets in advance (~€20–30).
Post-Golden Week mid-week: crowds are a fraction of summer peaks. Morning mist on the pillars is the defining image. Bring a warm layer — altitude gets cool in October. Book 魅力湘西 tickets before the trip; evening show usually 20:00 or 20:30.
